ABOUT CODE//WARS

We built this for people who learn by doing. Reading about algorithms only gets you so far — writing them is what makes them stick.

WHAT WE OFFER

  • A growing library of challenges, tagged by topic and difficulty.
  • An AI guru that hands you hints and questions, never working code.
  • Elo-matched duels that pit you against other devs in real time.
  • Community solutions, so you can compare your approach with others.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Pick a fight — a challenge that matches your level.
  2. Write your solution in the language you know best.
  3. Submit and iterate until every test case passes.
  4. Queue up and put the skill to work in a ranked duel.

Have an idea for a feature or a challenge you'd like to see? Open a channel.
